Privacy Policy

Last updated {{UPDATED}}

The short version. We collect what you type into our forms, plus basic technical details that help us block spam. We use it to run the waitlist, count votes, answer messages and tell you when pre-orders open. We don't sell it, we don't run ads, and we don't set tracking cookies. Email {{EMAIL}} and we'll delete your information.

Who we are

{{LEGAL_LINE}} In this policy, "we" and "us" mean {{LEGAL_NAME}}. You can reach us at {{EMAIL}}.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist: your email address and the answers you choose to give: what you're interested in, your look and base finish votes, where your HomeView will live, whether you have one and its color, what you think of our planned prices, and any note you write. We also record the referral code in the link you arrived from, if any, a random share code for your own link, how you first found us (the campaign tags in the link, such as utm_source, and the name of the website that sent you, not the full address) and the first page you landed on.

When you send us a message: your email address, your message and the page you sent it from.

With either form: your IP address, your browser type and how long the form was open, which we use to spot and block spam.

When you visit: our hosting provider keeps standard server records. {{ANALYTICS_LINE}}

On your device: the site saves your share code, whether you've joined, and how you first found us in your browser's local storage, so your share link stays the same and we can tell which links bring people in. This stays on your device until you sign up; then the share code and how you found us are sent with your sign-up. We don't set advertising or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider may set a strictly necessary security cookie to filter out bots.

How we use it

  • To run the waitlist and count votes and referrals.
  • To email you about pre-orders, launch dates and shipping. Every email will include a way to unsubscribe.
  • To answer your messages.
  • To block spam and keep the site secure.
  • To understand, in aggregate, which looks and products people want.

We don't sell your personal information, share it for advertising, or use it to build profiles of you.

Who helps us run the site

We use a few service providers. They handle your information only to provide their service to us.

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and provides the domain and email forwarding{{ANALYTICS_PROVIDER}}.
  • n8n receives form submissions, stores the waitlist and messages, and can send us an email notice of each new sign-up.
  • Google provides the inbox where our email, including those sign-up notices, is delivered.

When we start sending launch emails we may add an email delivery service, and we'll update this page to name it. If you use the "Save to Pinterest" or share buttons, the service you share to handles that under its own privacy policy. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep waitlist and message records only as long as we need them for the purposes above. If we decide not to launch, we'll delete the waitlist. If you ask us to delete your information, we will, unless the law requires us to keep it.

Your choices

Email {{EMAIL}} to see, correct or delete what we have about you, or to leave the waitlist. You can also unsubscribe from any email we send. Depending on where you live, you may have more rights under your state's law; we honor these requests for everyone.

Our site doesn't track you across other websites, so it doesn't respond differently to browser "Do Not Track" signals.

Children

This site is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Security and location

The site uses HTTPS, and only the people who run {{BRAND_HTML}} can see form submissions. No system is perfectly secure. We're based in the United States, and our providers may process information in the United States and other countries.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If a change materially affects information we already have, we'll tell the waitlist by email.
