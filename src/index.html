Questions

When does it ship?
HomeView arrives November 13. We test fit, sound and tilt on real units that week, then open pre-orders with real photos. We are aiming to ship the first drop in December, made in small batches.
Does the cover change the sound or block the camera?
Every frame is designed to leave the camera open, and the sweater is shaped to clear the speakers. We will measure sound with and without the cover on real units, and we will not ship a look that muffles it.
What is it made of?
Platinum-cure silicone for the frames, sweaters, toppers and charm loops, and aluminum or nylon for the base. The wood looks add a 3 mm face of real oak, walnut or maple, with a base topped in the same wood. No magnets, and nothing metal touches the screen.
Will it fit the wall-mounted HomeView?
The first frames are for the tabletop model. A wall version is on the list if enough of you ask for it.
Is this an Apple product?
No. The Snug Collection is an independent small business. We make accessories for Apple HomeView and are not affiliated with or endorsed by Apple.
Does joining the waitlist cost anything?
No. Joining is free and doesn't commit you to buy. You'll get an email when pre-orders open.
