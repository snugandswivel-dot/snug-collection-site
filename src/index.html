The swivel base

Face it where you are.

A slim lazy-Susan base for the tabletop HomeView. Turn the screen toward the stove, the sink or the table with one finger. The base stops at 170° each way so the power cable never wraps.

  • No metal, no magnets: nylon plates, an acetal ring and a silicone drag ring. Nothing near the speakers or the radios.
  • A silicone cup holds the foot; the speaker band stays fully open.
  • Pearl grey to start, with color-matched bases coming with later drops.
  • $49, or $72 with any cover. Free shipping on the set.

Questions

When does it ship?
HomeView arrives November 13. We test fit, sound and the hinge on real units that week, open pre-orders with real photos, and ship the first drop in December. Made in Georgia, in small batches.
Does the cover change the sound or block the camera?
The sweater stops above the speaker band and every frame leaves the camera strip open. We measure the sound with and without the cover, and we won't ship a look that changes it.
What is it made of?
Platinum-cure silicone for the frame and the cast sweaters, knit fabric for the rib and bouclé sweaters, and dyed nylon for the base. No metal and no magnets anywhere near the device.
Will it fit the wall-mounted HomeView?
The first frames are for the tabletop model. A wall version is on the list if enough of you ask for it.
Is this an Apple product?
No. The Snug Collection is an independent small business. We make accessories for Apple HomeView and are not affiliated with or endorsed by Apple.
Does joining the waitlist cost anything?
No. Joining is free and doesn't commit you to buy. You'll get an email when pre-orders open.
